BU SPARK!

Project Goal

The Problem: Massachusetts generates over 1 million tons of food waste annually. Simultaneously, food insecurity among households has risen to nearly 20%. BU Dining needs a way to distribute perfectly good catering leftovers before they expire and must be composted.

The Solution: A native mobile app (iOS/Android) that allows BU Dining staff to instantly post leftover events and notifies students immediately, creating a seamless pipeline from event to plate.

Client & Partners

Client: BU Dining Sustainability Team

Track: Spark! GreenField (DS519)

Supporting the Commonwealth's goal to eliminate commercial organic waste.

Process & Methods

  • Agile Development: Bi-weekly sprints to migrate web features to mobile native architecture.
  • UX Optimization: Streamlined the "Create Event" flow for time-sensitive catering staff.
  • Integration: Synchronized data between the legacy web dashboard and the new mobile app.

Reflections

Migrating from a web-app to React Native presented challenges in navigation routing and state management, but significantly improved the user experience for mobile-first students.

Next Steps: Expansion to the App Store and Google Play for campus-wide adoption.
