FreeBites

Catering Leftovers | BU Dining Sustainability

Project Goal

The Problem: Massachusetts generates over 1 million tons of food waste annually. Simultaneously, food insecurity among households has risen to nearly 20%.

The Solution: A native mobile app that allows BU Dining staff to instantly post leftover events and notifies students immediately. This creates a seamless pipeline from event to plate, reducing commercial organic waste.
